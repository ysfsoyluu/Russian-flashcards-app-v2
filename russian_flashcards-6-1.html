Information

What is Shuffle & Learn?

Shuffle & Learn is a Russian vocabulary app for all levels — A1 beginner to C2 advanced. Study 1,406 words with flashcards, Wordle and a fast-paced matching game.

How to use

Swipe right or tap Learned ✓ — you know this word.

Swipe left or tap Revisit ↺ — add it to your revisit pile.

Swipe up or tap Favourite ★ — save the word to My Lists.

Tap a card — flip it to see the translation and an example sentence.

Long-press Shuffle — open the level picker (A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1 · C2 · All).

Games

Wordle — guess a 5-letter Russian word in 6 tries.

Matching Game — race the clock to pair Russian words with their translations. Every match adds 3 seconds. Pairs appear with a delay so the board stays unpredictable.

Word count

Currently contains 1,406 words across A1–C2, covering everyday vocabulary through advanced academic and philosophical terms.
